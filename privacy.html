YOUR INFORMATION

Student privacy

AI WorkHub uses your information to manage applications, enrolment and access to learning. For questions or requests, contact us on WhatsApp: 0742 330 046.

What we use

Google sign-in supplies account information including your name, email and account identifier. You may instead create an email-and-password account. Supabase Auth processes your password, verification and reset requests; the application does not store your password in student records. We use your name and email to identify your student account; we do not receive your Google password. Your application adds your phone number and payment-plan choice. Verified payment records, enrolment status, admission numbers, admission letters, assignment files and notes, learning progress and released certificates are associated with your account.

Why we use it

We use these details to review your application, contact you about the programme, manage verified payments and provide appropriate access to learning resources and certificates. Selecting a payment plan does not process a payment.

Services and access

Google handles Google sign-in, Supabase provides authentication, database and private file storage, GitHub Pages hosts the website, and, when configured, Resend delivers email confirmations, password resets and admission-letter emails with PDF attachments addressed to your verified account email. These services may process data outside Kenya. Your student data is restricted to your account and authorised operators, subject to the service providers’ processing. We do not publish applications or payment records on the website.

Sessions on your device

The portal stores authentication session information in your browser to keep you signed in. On a shared device, sign out when finished. This portal does not add advertising trackers.

Your choices and requests

You can contact AI WorkHub to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information. We may need to verify your identity and retain records where required by law or to resolve an outstanding enrolment or payment matter. Do not send passwords or sensitive identity documents through the application form.
